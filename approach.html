Approach

A Salutogenic Approach

The art of healing is to nourish the body, calm the mind and uplift the soul.

The clinic follows a salutogenic perspective: rather than focusing exclusively on illness or symptoms, it also explores the conditions that help health, vitality and resilience develop.

Guiding Principles

Supporting health, not only treating illness

Attention is given to what strengthens a person, alongside what troubles them.

Working with rhythm, warmth, touch and awareness

These are the classical means of anthroposophic external therapy, applied gently and attentively.

Recognizing the individual as a whole person

Biography, environment and inner life all belong to the clinical picture.

Encouraging active participation

Healing is something a person does, supported by the therapist rather than performed upon them.

Combining approaches thoughtfully

Complementary modalities are added only where they genuinely serve the person in front of us.

How the work fits together

Medical consultation
Complementary therapies, chosen for you
Anthroposophic external therapies

Every path begins with a medical consultation. Anthroposophic external therapies form the core of the treatment. Complementary approaches are added around that core, only where they suit you and your situation.

Alongside conventional medicine

Anthroposophic medicine extends conventional medicine; it does not replace it. Where conventional diagnosis, investigation or treatment is needed, it is recommended, and collaboration with your treating physician is welcomed.
