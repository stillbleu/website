Consultation & booking

Find a time for your consultation

Appointments offer a quiet and individualized space to explore your needs and identify an appropriate therapeutic pathway.

What happens in a first consultation

The first appointment lasts an hour. It opens with about twenty minutes of conversation — what brings you, your medical history, how you are living at the moment, and a clinical examination where that is useful. The remaining forty minutes are given to a first treatment, chosen together from what the conversation brings to light. You leave having already begun, and with a sense of what a longer path might look like.

What to Expect

  • Initial consultation and personal assessment
  • Discussion of current concerns, lifestyle and goals
  • Recommendation of suitable anthroposophic external therapies
  • Integration of complementary approaches where appropriate
  • Follow-up appointments to support continuity

How Booking Works

  1. Select the type of appointment.
  2. Choose an available date and time in the calendar.
  3. Enter your contact details.
  4. Confirm the booking.
  5. Receive an email confirmation.
