Getting Here

By car
About 10 minutes from Neuchâtel: take motorway exit 9 and keep following the direction of Pontarlier. In the first stretch of forest after Corcelles, turn right onto the small road up to Montézillon (NE).
By public transport
From Neuchâtel train station, take the postal bus 380 in the direction of “Le Locle” — the stop is at the top of the escalators, right-hand exit, on the lake side — and get off at the Montmollin stop. From there, walk toward the old train station and follow the path along the railway line. Once you reach the level crossing, take the sidewalk and head down the road; you will find me on your right, just after the small parking place.
Parking
Free parking on site, in front of the hotel and restaurant.
