Philosophy

The idea behind StillBleu

StillBleu means taking a pause: a moment to slow down, listen and reconnect with what supports your health.

My intention is to offer care that is medically responsible, personally attentive and open to the many dimensions of wellbeing. Together, we can explore practical ways to support your health, vitality and inner balance.

Consultations are available in English and French.

About

Dr Srijan Gupta

Medical Doctor & Anthroposophic Physician

My name is Dr Srijan Gupta. I am a medical doctor (MBBS, GGSIP University, New Delhi) with clinical experience in conventional medicine and additional training in anthroposophic medicine, acupuncture and complementary approaches to health and wellbeing.

At the heart of my work is the belief that every person deserves to be listened to with care and understood as an individual. I take time to consider not only physical symptoms, but also your emotional wellbeing, daily life, stress levels, patterns of vitality and the circumstances that may be influencing your health.

Anthroposophic medicine extends conventional medicine by looking at the whole person and the relationship between body, soul and spirit. It does not replace conventional medicine; rather, it offers an additional perspective that can complement medical care.

This background shapes a practice that takes the whole person seriously while remaining grounded in careful clinical observation, medical responsibility and genuine human connection.

Internationally accredited as an anthroposophic physician by the Medical Section at the Goetheanum, after completing three years of training at Emerson College, UK.

True healing arises from treating the whole person: body, mind, emotions and environment.

My journey

My medical training gave me a strong foundation in the diagnosis, treatment and prevention of illness. Through my clinical experience, I became increasingly interested in the many factors that influence health beyond symptoms alone — rest, stress, lifestyle, emotional resilience, recovery and our relationship with ourselves.

I began to explore how these dimensions can be acknowledged within a responsible and individualised approach to healthcare.

This led me towards anthroposophic medicine and other complementary disciplines. These approaches offered me further ways of understanding and supporting people, while reinforcing something that has always been important to me: the value of listening carefully, meeting each person where they are and respecting their individual path.

StillBleu grew from this journey. It is a place where medical knowledge and holistic care can meet in a calm, attentive and personal setting — a space where you can pause, reflect and explore what may support your health and wellbeing.

How I work

A consultation begins with listening.

We take time to understand what has brought you to StillBleu, your medical history, your current concerns, your lifestyle and the wider circumstances that may be affecting your health.

You do not need to have everything clearly defined before you come. Sometimes the first step is simply having the time and space to describe how you have been feeling.

The approach is adapted to you. There is no single method that is right for everyone, and not every therapy is appropriate in every situation.

The aim is not to replace conventional medical care, but to complement it. When appropriate, I may recommend that you consult another healthcare professional, seek further medical assessment or continue treatment with your existing medical team.

My intention is to offer care that is attentive, grounded and respectful, combining medical knowledge with warmth, curiosity and a sincere interest in your individual experience.

Depending on your needs, a consultation may include:

  • A detailed medical history and careful clinical assessment
  • Discussion of your current symptoms, concerns and personal goals
  • An anthroposophic perspective on your overall health and constitution
  • Practical guidance related to rest, stress, lifestyle and self-care
  • Acupuncture or other appropriate therapeutic approaches
  • Anthroposophic external therapies
  • Selected complementary practices to support relaxation, awareness and wellbeing

Clinical Experience

Before moving to Switzerland I spent several years in hospital medicine in New Delhi: an internship at Safdarjung Hospital, emergency medicine at Bhagwan Mahavir Hospital, and work at the National Institute of Tuberculosis and Respiratory Diseases. I then worked as holistic health consultant and assistant medical superintendent at Lotus Hospital, alongside consultancy on health and sustainability with The Energy and Resources Institute and work with the V-Excel Education Trust in Chennai. Since 2026 I have practised at L'Aubier in Montézillon (NE).
